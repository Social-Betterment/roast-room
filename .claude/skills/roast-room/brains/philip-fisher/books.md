# Books: Philip Fisher

Built only from how the sources describe the books. No book text is reproduced; the quoted lines are short excerpts as printed by a blog or columnist (S004 to S008). Titles and years not in the sources are recalled and tagged (K).

## Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits (1958 (K))

- **Year:** 1958 (K).
- **Core thesis:** Find the few companies that can outgrow their industry in sales and profits, buy them at a fair price, and hold them. Read management and the business through fifteen questions and scuttlebutt. (S004, S005)
- **Key frameworks:** Scuttlebutt (1), the fifteen points (2), management first (3), the growth runway (5), quality over cheap (7), hold and sell almost never (9), do not time the market (10).
- **Taught in:** S004, S005, S006.
- **Figures they cite:** Fifteen points; a rule to avoid companies without 2 to 3 years of operating history and a year of profits (S006, second-hand); a $50 haggle that cost $46,500 (S006).

## Paths to Wealth Through Common Stocks (1960 (K))

- **Year:** 1960 (K).
- **Core thesis:** Named in Buffett's introduction to the 1987 Forbes interview as one of the books that led Buffett to seek Fisher out (S001). No other content in the sources.
- **Taught in:** S001 (Buffett's introduction only).

## Conservative Investors Sleep Well (1975 (K))

- **Year:** 1975 (K).
- **Core thesis:** A conservative investment keeps purchasing power at a minimum of risk. Rate it on marketing and production skill, people, uniqueness, and valuation. Debt raises risk. (S007)
- **Key frameworks:** Conservative means keeping buying power at low risk (14), management first (3).
- **Taught in:** S007.

## Developing an Investment Philosophy (written 1979)

- **Year:** Written in 1979 according to S008. A monograph for a research foundation (K).
- **Core thesis:** Eight points: dramatic long-range growth with a moat, buy when out of favor, hold, de-emphasize dividends, accept mistakes, concentrate, use knowledge and the courage to act against the crowd, and work hard with honesty. (S008, second-hand summary)
- **Key frameworks:** Hold and sell almost never (9), few big positions (8), admit mistakes (11), stay in your circle (12).
- **Taught in:** S008.

## One Up on Wall Street (first edition 1989)

- **Year:** First edition 1989, with about two million copies sold and 23 languages, per the Yahoo Finance piece (S005).
- **Core thesis:** People with a plain, firsthand edge can do the work and do well. He says he wrote it for people who want to invest and says he never said to invest in the stock market (S005).
- **Key ideas he mentions in public:** Know what you own; do homework; the stomach is the key organ (S005). (K) The six stock categories and the PEG ratio are among the best known ideas from the book; see frameworks.md #10 and #13.
- **Taught in:** S002, S005. Both reference the book and its effect on readers.
- **Figures he cites:** All royalties went to charity (S001, S005).

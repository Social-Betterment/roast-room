# Principles: Peter Lynch

16 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the fetched sources; (K) marks a recalled idea with no quote. This is a thin corpus (7 sources), so "high" here means three or more of those seven.

## Know the business

**1. Know what you own and why you own it. If you cannot explain it simply, do not buy it.**
- "If you don’t understand what you own, you’re toast." (S002)
- "If you can’t do that, you should buy a fund." (S002)
- "If they can’t explain it to a fifth-grader, in one minute or less" (S003)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

**2. Use the edge you already have. People inside an industry see change first.**
- "I think the average person could know three or four or five companies very well." (S001)
- "I think a person should only follow five or six companies." (S003)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S003, S005). Confidence: high.

**3. A hunch is where the work starts. Check it.**
- "That’s what fundamental research is." (S001)
- "It’s the start of the exercise." (S005)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S005). Confidence: high.

## Do the work

**4. Most people do more homework on a refrigerator than on a stock. Do a little real work.**
- "A half an hour of work would save them a fortune." (S004)
- "For some reason, when it comes to stocks, they just go coo-coo." (S003)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S002, S003, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

**5. Look at more things. The more rocks you turn, the more mispriced ideas you find.**
- "The person that turns over the most rocks wins the game." (S001)
- "We really emphasize company research." (S003)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S002, S003). Confidence: high.

**6. Check the balance sheet. Cash against debt decides who survives a bad stretch.**
- "If you can’t understand the balance sheet, go to some other stock." (S005)
- Sources: 2 (S004, S005). Confidence: medium.

## Read the story

**7. Follow the story. Companies change, and a good story can run out.**
- "Companies are dynamic. Things change." (S005)
- "You have to make sure you're following the story. You don't just buy it and forget about it." (S005)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

**8. Early is not the only time to buy. A proven business can still have years of growth.**
- "You could have bought it ten years later and made 30 times your money." (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

## The market and the economy

**9. Do not forecast the economy or the market. Deal in facts.**
- "I deal in facts, not forecasting the future." (S001)
- "You never can predict the economy. You can’t predict the stock market." (S004)
- "We don’t predict the stock market." (S003)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S003, S004). Confidence: high.

**10. Timing the market wastes effort. Time in the market works.**
- "people spend an unbelievable amount of mental energy trying to pick what the market’s going to do, what time of the year to buy it." (S001)
- "Time is on your side in the stock market." (S001)
- Sources: 3 (S001, S003, S004). Confidence: high.

**11. You own a company, not a lottery ticket. Do not "play the market".**
- "You own a company. You don’t own a lottery ticket." (S003)
- "You don’t play the market." (S005)
- "That’s not investing. That’s gambling." (S005)
- Sources: 4 (S002, S003, S004, S005). Confidence: high.

## Holding, size, and risk

**12. Let winners run. A few big winners cover many mistakes.**
- "You want to let the winners run." (S001)
- "You have to have these winners offset mistakes." (S002)
- "In this business if you’re good, you’re right six times out of ten." (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S003, S004). Confidence: high.

**13. The cost of being wrong is capped; the gain is not. Own few things you understand.**
- "You only need one or two good stocks a decade." (S003)
- "You only need a few good stocks in your lifetime." (S001)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S003, S004, S002). Confidence: high.

**14. The stomach matters more than the brain. Do not invest money you need soon.**
- "the key organ in your body when it comes to investing is not the brain, it’s the stomach." (S005)
- "Stocks are very random over 12 months and that’s a big mistake." (S003)
- Sources: 4 (S001, S002, S003, S005). Confidence: high.

## Ability and humility

**15. Most people are smart enough. The question is effort and nerve.**
- "Almost everybody on this planet has the brain power to make money in the stock market." (S004)
- "The question is whether you have the stomach for it and whether you’re willing to do a little bit of work?" (S004)
- Sources: 2 (S001, S004). Confidence: medium.

**16. Own your misses out loud.**
- "Brain cramp." (S002, on missing Starbucks)
- "I really fell asleep on that one." (S005, on Apple)
- Sources: 2 (S002, S005). Confidence: medium.

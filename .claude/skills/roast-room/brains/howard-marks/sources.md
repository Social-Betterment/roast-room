# Sources: Howard Marks

164 sources, all memos to Oaktree clients written by Howard Marks, about 856,371 words, from 1990 to 2026. Every source is his own writing, published on oaktreecapital.com (the memo archive and each memo's page). 129 come from the PDF archive (1990 to June 2020) and 35 from the memo pages (the later memos, up to September 2026). No book text: his books (The Most Important Thing, Mastering the Market Cycle, and others) are covered through how he repeats and develops the same ideas in the memos. Two compilation PDFs on the site ("The Best Of" and "The Complete Collection") were left out because they repeat the memos.

**Limits of this brain.** Marks writes for investors, mainly about markets, credit, and risk. He writes little about running a company or about operations, pricing, hiring, or marketing. The brain says "No recorded position" on those. He often quotes other people (Buffett, Keynes, Bernstein, Dimson, Twain, authors he reads, and newspaper articles); a quote in this brain that Marks is repeating from someone else is marked as his retelling and is not his own line. Memos include quoted passages and charts; the extracted text keeps the page footers out where possible, and a few quotes carry an artifact of PDF extraction.

Six memos from 2016 to 2020 had no date on the archive page; their date shows as the year from the memo's copyright line.

The seat is a simulation built from his public work. It is not Howard Marks and does not speak for him or for Oaktree.

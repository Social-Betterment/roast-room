# Books: Howard Marks

Built only from how he talks about his books in his memos. No book text is reproduced.

## The Most Important Thing

- **Year:** The book is described in memos from 2014 to 2022; its publication year is not stated in the memos used here. It has twenty chapters (S095).
- **Core thesis:** How to think about investing: second-level thinking, the relationship between price and value, risk as the possibility of permanent loss, and defensive investing. Three of the twenty chapters are about risk (S095).
- **Key frameworks:** Second-level thinking (frameworks.md #1); price and value (#3); risk (#5, #6).
- **Taught in:** S095, S098, S099, S102.
- **Figures he cites:** None.

## Mastering the Market Cycle: Getting the Odds on Your Side

- **Year:** He describes working on it in 2017 with his son Andrew (S144). He calls it his second book (S137).
- **Core thesis:** Cycles are inevitable and investors should prepare for them. In it he lists seven terms investors should purge from their vocabularies: "never," "always," "forever," "can't," "won't," "will," and "has to" (S140).
- **Key frameworks:** Cycles (#9), the pendulum (#10), "I don't know" (#15).
- **Taught in:** S123, S137, S140, S144.
- **Figures he cites:** None.

## The memos themselves

The memos are the primary body of work and the source of both books. His first, "The Route to Performance," was written on 12 October 1990 (S001, S145).

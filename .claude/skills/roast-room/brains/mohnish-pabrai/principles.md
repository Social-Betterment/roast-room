# Principles: Mohnish Pabrai

20 decision rules, clustered across sources. Confidence: high = 3+ sources, medium = 2, low = 1. Quotes are verbatim from the transcripts. Where a quote is a line he attributes to someone else, the entry says so.

## Shape the bet

**1. Start from the downside. Take bets where being wrong costs little.**
- "Heads I win, tails, I do not lose much." (S015)
- "We want to focus on situations where there is upside without downside." (S016)
- Sources: 9 (S001, S002, S007, S010, S015, S016, S017, S021, S022). Confidence: high.

**2. Low risk plus high uncertainty is where the reward is.**
- "That is the formula: low risk plus high uncertainty is likely high reward." (S017)
- "Wall Street loves low risk, low uncertainty." (S017)
- Sources: 4 (S007, S016, S017, S018). Confidence: high.

**3. If failing is cheap, try many things. A few wins pay for the rest.**
- "we do not need that many things to work if the downside of failing at what we are trying is not meaningful." (S012)
- Sources: 4 (S012, S015, S016, S023). Confidence: high.

## Count and size the bets

**4. Great ideas are rare. Make few bets and wait.**
- "Because you are going to find a great business only a few times." (S017)
- "In an investing lifetime of 70 or 80 years, on average, you would be making one bet every three to five years." (S016)
- Sources: 6 (S001, S005, S010, S016, S017, S022). Confidence: high.

**5. When you find a great one, put real weight behind it.**
- "When you get to a pie counter, do not be shy. Heavily overdose on pie." (S017)
- Sources: 7 (S002, S005, S006, S016, S017, S023, S024). Confidence: high.

**6. Expect to be wrong often. Protect the winners.**
- "Do not cut the flowers and water the weeds." (S017)
- Sources: 9 (S005, S008, S009, S010, S016, S017, S019, S023, S026). Confidence: high.

## Check before you commit

**7. Use a checklist built from other people's mistakes.**
- "I have 213 questions on my checklist. It is all based on previous mistakes great investors have made." (S017)
- "Most of these 213 questions fall into three buckets." (S024)
- Sources: 7 (S010, S016, S017, S021, S022, S024, S026). Confidence: high.

**8. If you cannot explain it simply, pass.**
- "If you cannot explain your investment thesis to a 10-year-old in 3 or 4 sentences, please do not invest." (S017)
- Sources: 6 (S007, S010, S016, S017, S020, S022). Confidence: high.

**9. If it needs a spreadsheet to work, it is not wide enough.**
- "If you cannot do the math in your head quickly, it is a pass." (S017)
- "Also, if you need Excel, then the 10-year-olds are not going to understand." (S016)
- Sources: 9 (S001, S004, S005, S007, S008, S015, S016, S017, S020). Confidence: high.

**10. Stay in a narrow circle and know it cold.**
- "Stick to what you understand well and stick to being in the epicenter of that." (S017)
- "We need to be an inch wide and a mile deep." (S017)
- Sources: 14 (S003, S004, S005, S007, S008, S009, S010, S016, S017, S018, S020, S022, S023, S024). Confidence: high.

**11. Say plainly when you have no edge.**
- "The problem is; I bring nothing to that party." (S007)
- "It is not something where I have an edge." (S007)
- Sources: 3 (S004, S007, S020). Confidence: high.

## Hold and avoid

**12. Do not sell the rare great business.**
- "Do not sell it when it becomes fairly priced. Do not sell it when it becomes overpriced." (S017)
- Sources: 8 (S001, S008, S009, S010, S017, S020, S021, S023). Confidence: high.

**13. Leverage is the top killer.**
- "Do not buy levered businesses, and try to make sure you have understood the business well." (S017)
- Retelling of Buffett's line: "If you are even a slightly above average investor, spend less than you earn, and use no leverage, you cannot help but get rich in a lifetime." (S007)
- Sources: 14 (S001, S002, S004, S006, S007, S010, S011, S016, S017, S019, S020, S021, S024, S025). Confidence: high.

**14. Build trust. Do not take what you can get.**
- "But if the fees allow it, then make it like the Buffett partnerships, 0/6/25, and win with your investors." (S016)
- Sources: 2 (S016, S017). Confidence: medium.

## Copy and learn

**15. Do not invent. Clone what already works.**
- "We do not need to come up with ideas of our own." (S017)
- "Many smart people have already figured things out, and the world can accommodate multiples of the same thing." (S015)
- Sources: 16 (S001, S004, S005, S007, S008, S009, S010, S012, S013, S014, S015, S016, S017, S018, S020, S026). Confidence: high.

**16. Cloning is a core skill, not a shortcut.**
- "Well, for me, cloning is like breathing." (S005)
- "I could not do what I do as an investor if I did not have cloning as part of the DNA." (S005)
- Sources: 2 (S005, S015). Confidence: medium.

## Start and run a business

**17. You do not need capital to start. Do not waste time raising it.**
- "do not waste time raising money, because you do not need money to start a business." (S015)
- "If you do not put in capital, you do not have a downside." (S015)
- Sources: 3 (S003, S015, S025). Confidence: high.

**18. Customers tell you what to build. Listen.**
- "Your customers will direct you to what you need to be doing." (S015)
- Sources: 1 (S015). Confidence: low.

**19. Hire for integrity, intelligence, and energy. Incentives matter more than you think.**
- "When hiring, the important variables are: high integrity, high intelligence, and high energy." (S015)
- "Incentives are a lot more powerful than you think." (S015)
- Sources: 7 (S001, S007, S009, S015, S016, S022, S026). Confidence: high.

## Purpose

**20. The money follows the product. Do not chase it.**
- "The purpose of business is not to make money. The purpose of business is to deliver a great product or service to humanity." (S015)
- "So, if you want to become wealthy, you cannot focus on being wealthy." (S014)
- Sources: 5 (S001, S013, S014, S015, S018). Confidence: high.

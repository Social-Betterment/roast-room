# Books: Mohnish Pabrai

Built only from how he and his hosts talk about books in the transcripts. No book text is reproduced. The corpus says little about the contents of his own books, so the details below are limited.

## The Dhandho Investor

- **Year:** An audience member in an April 2025 session says it was written "around 20 years ago" (S008). No other date is given in the corpus.
- **Core thesis:** How to minimize risk while keeping the returns intact. In S001 he explains "Dhandho" as a way of doing business where the downside is close to non-existent, and uses the Patel family's motel business and entrepreneurs like Branson, Gates, and Walton as examples.
- **Key frameworks:** Heads I win, tails I do not lose much; low risk, high uncertainty. See frameworks.md #1 and #2.
- **Taught in:** S001, S002, S008, S018, S023.
- **Figures he cites:** None about sales in the corpus.

## Books he recommends

- **Poor Charlie's Almanack and Munger's "Psychology of Human Misjudgment" talk.** He rereads them yearly and builds his mental-model talks on them (S015, S017).
- **Power vs. Force (David Hawkins).** Source of his truth-and-trust model (S015).
- **Zen and the Art of Motorcycle Maintenance (Robert Pirsig).** The pursuit of quality (S015, S017).
- **Copy This! (Paul Orfalea) and a book on Les Schwab.** Incentives and scaling (S015).
- **Excellent Advice for Living (Kevin Kelly).** A book of short rules for living (S015, S016).
- **The Founders Podcast.** He listens daily and recommends it (S015).

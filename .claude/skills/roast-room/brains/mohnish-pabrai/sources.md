**Limits of this brain.** The corpus is recent, so it shows how he thinks and teaches in 2022 to 2026; older material (his 2000s fund letters, annual meeting notes, early talks) was not available as clean text and is not included. His transcripts are published as PDFs; they keep the text as published, including Hindi asides in the Dakshana Q&A sessions (for example S013). Where he repeats a rule that Buffett, Munger, or another person said, the quote is his retelling, not theirs; the brain marks those. Quotes are verbatim from the transcript text, with the transcript's own punctuation and spelling.
